# Changelog

## 1.1.1

* The timeline, dope sheet, NLA, video sequencer and movie clip editors follow the theme: their backgrounds, text and grid were left in Blender grey. Their strip, keyframe and marker colors keep their Blender defaults.

## 1.1.0

The four themes are repainted to match their references more closely: two pieces of key art from a certain game set somewhere in sunny Florida, whose name shall not be spoken.

* Bay Light: the bay in daylight. Sky blue, mint water and sand, with a sky to mint water gradient in the 3D viewport.
* Bay: the same bay at nightfall. Deep water blues, a lilac evening sky and selected objects in peach.
* Sunset Light: the sky at dusk, from periwinkle and lavender to pink and peach.
* Sunset: plum instead of navy, a periwinkle to dusky rose gradient and selected objects in peach.
* Light themes: in Blender 5.3, the text of the top bar and the status bar is dark and readable again.

## 1.0.0

* Four themes for Blender 4.2 and later: Miami '26 Bay, Bay Light, Sunset and Sunset Light, each as its own extension.
