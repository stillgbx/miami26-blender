- **Release notes** (1.1.0): `Repainted to match its reference more closely: key art from a certain game set somewhere in sunny Florida, whose name shall not be spoken. In light themes, the top bar and status bar text is readable again in Blender 5.3.`

## Miami '26 Bay

Featured image and preview: `Preview/listing/bay.png`

```markdown
Miami colors for Blender: the sky blue, turquoise water, lavender palm trees and peach facades of a day on the bay, here at nightfall.

- The whole interface in deep water blues and lilac evening sky, with a turquoise accent.
- An evening sky gradient in the 3D viewport, from lilac to deep water.
- Selected objects in peach.
- The text editor and the Python console in the Miami '26 syntax colors.
- Colors that carry a meaning keep their Blender defaults: axes, curve handles, bones, collections, strips, node categories and animation states.

Part of Miami '26: Bay and Sunset, each in dark and light. Also available for Visual Studio and VS Code.
```
